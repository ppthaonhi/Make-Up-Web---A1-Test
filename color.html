THE BASICS

What is personal color?

It is a way to explore how color qualities interact with your natural coloring.

01

Undertone

A subtle warm, cool, or balanced quality beneath the surface of your skin. It is different from surface redness and does not change with a tan.

02

Value

How light or deep a color feels. Some people enjoy lighter palettes, others feel at home in deeper shades, and many wear both.

03

Chroma

How clear or muted a color appears. Compare vivid shades with softer, dustier versions to notice which feels harmonious to you.

04

Contrast

The difference between light and dark features in your overall appearance. Contrast can guide how bold or soft a makeup look feels.

The four seasonal families

Spring

Often warm, light, and clear. Try peach, coral, warm pink, and fresh green.

Summer

Often cool, light, and soft. Try dusty rose, mauve, lavender, and cool blue.

Autumn

Often warm, deep, and muted. Try terracotta, olive, cinnamon, and warm brown.

Winter

Often cool, deep, and clear. Try berry, jewel tones, crisp navy, and cool red.

Seasonal systems are simplified guides. Lighting, personal taste, culture, and styling all affect what colors you enjoy wearing.
